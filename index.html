Android app

Emergency First Aid Guide

Type what happened in your own words, like "my friend burned his hand", and the app shows clear, numbered steps for that emergency, with a button to call 140.

Every step uses things people already have at home, such as t-shirts, blankets and towels, and none of them involve giving medicine. It works fully offline: a text matcher I wrote handles typos, word forms and long sentences, so there's no internet or AI that could make up advice.

  • Covers20 emergencies, from choking and bleeding to stroke and drowning
  • Visuals60 illustrations drawn as vector graphics
  • Built withKotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3
